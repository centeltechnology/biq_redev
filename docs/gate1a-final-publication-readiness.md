### Locks, monitoring, and abort

The nullable/no-default column avoids a heap rewrite, but ALTER TABLE takes an
ACCESS EXCLUSIVE baker lock; CHECK validation can require scanning. Foreign-key
creation also locks the referenced table. Locks can last until transaction end.
The ordinary index is created on an empty new table. No data backfill is needed.

Choose low traffic and confirm the primary has no long-running baker
transactions. A primary-authorized operator can observe `pg_stat_activity`,
`pg_locks`, and `pg_blocking_pids` without exposing SQL text/customer information.
The replica observation cannot substitute for this live-primary monitoring.

Before publication, the owner must establish the platform's migration
transaction boundaries, finite timeout or equivalent bounded-wait policy, who
can observe the primary, and who can cancel an unsafe migration. No verified
in-flight migration-cancellation control is available to this agent.
