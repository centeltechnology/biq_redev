Please make a focused UX/content pass across BakerIQ’s onboarding, first-dashboard experience, and Payments page to make our new **BakerIQ Payments** positioning consistent everywhere.

The underlying Stripe Connect implementation has already been established and should NOT be redesigned.

## Primary objective

We have changed our payment positioning from:

**“Connect Stripe”**

to:

**“Set Up BakerIQ Payments”**

Stripe remains the infrastructure and should be disclosed clearly as:

**Powered by Stripe**

However, bakers should primarily experience this as setting up BakerIQ’s payment functionality and unlocking more ways for their customers to pay.

Make the following changes as one coordinated update.

---

# 1. Preserve the existing payment architecture

Do NOT modify:

* Stripe Connect account architecture
* Direct-charge model
* Application/platform fee logic
* Free / Basic / Pro transaction fees
* Merchant-of-record structure
* Baker responsibility for refunds/disputes
* Negative-balance responsibility
* Payout routing
* Webhook architecture
* Subscription billing
* Existing Stripe account onboarding requirements

This task is primarily UX, terminology, navigation, payment-method configuration, and analytics.

Do not convert the payment architecture to destination charges or separate charges/transfers.

---

Search the application for baker-facing references such as:

* Connect Stripe
* Stripe Setup
* Enable Stripe
* Stripe Payments
* Stripe Connected
* Stripe not connected
* Payments will appear once Stripe is connected

Review each instance.

Where the baker is being asked to enable or use our payment functionality, prefer:

**BakerIQ Payments**

Examples:

**Connect Stripe**

Use:

**Set Up BakerIQ Payments**

Instead of:

**Accept deposits so customers can pay online — Connect Stripe**

**Accept deposits and give customers more ways to pay**

**Cards, Cash App Pay, Apple Pay, Link and more. Powered by Stripe.**

Instead of:

**Payments will appear here once Stripe is connected.**

**Payments will appear here once BakerIQ Payments is set up.**

IMPORTANT:

Do not remove Stripe disclosure.

Where appropriate, show:

**Powered by Stripe**

or:

**Secure payment processing powered by Stripe**

The goal is not to hide Stripe. The goal is to distinguish the baker-facing product from the underlying payment processor.

**“Look what you just unlocked.”**

It should NOT feel like:

**“Complete another setup form.”**

If Stripe payment-method configuration cannot be loaded for some reason, do not block onboarding.

Show a message such as:

**Your payment setup is complete. You can review payment methods later from Payments.**

Then allow the baker to continue.
